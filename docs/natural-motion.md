# Locomotion and side-on tackles

Walk, jog and sprint blend stride cadence, elbow flexion, hip sway and posture.
Turns feed a smoothed lean from current heading error and speed; acceleration
leans also blend rather than snapping. Per-athlete style and per-cycle variation
are bounded and smoothed. Physical foot contact endpoints remain authoritative.

A slide chooses its extended leg from the ball's approach side and tucks the
other leg. The pelvis rolls onto the supporting hip while maintaining its
centre over the physical body. Both the ball sweep and player contacts use the
chosen visible boot. Boot soles compensate pelvis roll to retain ground-facing
orientation. Ground friction controls travel; the action cannot steer or regain
lost momentum.

Recovery plants the supporting hand and folds the extended leg into a planted
stance, then rolls the pelvis back and pushes up through a crouch. The body
collider grows with that recovery instead of instantly becoming full height.
Agility affects recovery duration. A physical impact can interrupt the slide
and send the player into the existing ragdoll recovery.

The focused off-screen fixture covers all six athletes through walking,
jogging, sprinting, turns/stops, dribbling, left/right slides, a miss and an
interrupted slide. Rendered frames and actual boot, hand and hip positions
support visual evaluation; the fixture does not establish subjective realism
or comprehensive full-match quality.
